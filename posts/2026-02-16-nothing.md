none of this means anything

the [money isn't real](https://wtfhappenedin1971.com/), [the politics aren't real](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/19/January_2025_Official_Presidential_Portrait_of_Donald_J._Trump.jpg/1280px-January_2025_Official_Presidential_Portrait_of_Donald_J._Trump.jpg), [your job isn't real](https://notrelated.xyz/audio/s02/S02E06_-_The_Economy_is_Fake_the_Jobs_are_Fake_the_Money_is_Fake.ogg), the images aren't real, the thoughts aren't real, [the growth isn't real](https://youtu.be/5WPB2u8EzL8?si=gYXs4DnWJ_Nx6-5M&t=2832)

is anything real? are you?

and how would you know if you are real? is your body strong? is your mind sharp? how are your neighbors doing? how are your parents doing? what are you afraid of? what do you like to do? what makes you mad?

are you listening to people who know you exist? are you taking advice from people who like you?

does all of this have to come crashing down for the madness to end?
